## Q3 FY26
### 1. Repeat & Retention Metrics
- **Revenue retention:** steady revenue retention *(Q3 FY26, shareholder_letter, p.6)* — *"steady revenue retention"*

### 2. Core Consumer Services vs. InstaHelp
- InstaHelp serves high‑frequency daily/weekly cleaning and cooking, while core consumer services focus on lower‑frequency deep cleaning *(Q3 FY26, earnings_transcript, p.8)* — *"InstaHelp caters to a more high‑frequency daily/ weekly cleaning and housekeeping service... core consumer services... deep cleaning"*
- No meaningful cannibalisation observed yet between InstaHelp and core services *(Q3 FY26, earnings_transcript, p.8)* — *"Nothing meaningful yet"*

### 3. Category & Seasonality Patterns
- India Consumer Services (ex InstaHelp) shows clear seasonality with stronger demand in Q1 (summer) and Q3 (Diwali/festive) *(Q3 FY26, shareholder_letter, p.13)* — *"India Consumer Services (Ex InstaHelp) - Exhibits clear seasonality with stronger consumer demand in Q1 (summer) and Q3 (Diwali and festive season)"*
- InstaHelp currently shows no significant seasonality *(Q3 FY26, shareholder_letter, p.13)* — *"InstaHelp is in its early phases of development, and as it evolves, we will have a better view on its inherent seasonality"*

### 4. Cities & Customer Segments
- InstaHelp is live in major metropolitan cities: Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad and parts of Pune *(Q3 FY26, earnings_transcript, p.12)* — *"currently live in the major metropolitan cities, which is Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, and parts of Pune"*
- InstaHelp targets urban middle‑income households seeking on‑demand short‑duration help *(Q3 FY26, shareholder_letter, p.20)* — *"A growing share of urban households, particularly in metros are seeking reliable, flexible, and on‑demand alternatives to traditional full‑time house help"*

### 5. Customer Experience & Product Levers
- Better and more reliable customer experience (faster fulfilment, consistent quality, fair pricing) is a key growth driver for core services *(Q3 FY26, shareholder_letter, p.14)* — *"Better and more reliable customer experience: Faster service fulfilment, consistent quality, and fair pricing will improve customer satisfaction and repeat usage"*
- InstaHelp drives higher engagement and cross‑category trials, supporting spend per user growth *(Q3 FY26, shareholder_letter, p.14)* — *"InstaHelp is expected to drive higher engagement and cross‑category trials over time, supporting improvements in overall spend per user"*

### 6. Gaps / Blind Spots (What Filings Do NOT Explain)
- Management does not disclose quantitative repeat‑purchase rates, churn percentages, or exact cross‑category utilisation metrics for InstaHelp versus core services, leaving those figures to be uncovered via customer reviews and interviews.
- No specific data is provided on city‑level coverage completeness or the number of micro‑markets tested, so interview insights are needed to gauge true geographic penetration.
- Exact ordering frequency (e.g., average orders per user per month) and stickiness of service professionals are not quantified, requiring primary research to assess user and partner retention.

## Q4 FY26
### 1. Repeat & Retention Metrics
- **InstaHelp monthly user retention *(excluded category — NOT core services)*:** 30-33% *(Q4 FY26, shareholder_letter, p.13)* — *"InstaHelp is showing strong early signs of user retention and repeat frequency. Monthly user retention is between 30-33%"*
- **InstaHelp orders per retained user per month *(excluded category)*:** around 2.5 orders per month *(Q4 FY26, shareholder_letter, p.13)* — *"retained users place around 2.5 orders per month"*
- **InstaHelp top 10% monthly users orders in March 2026 *(excluded category)*:** around 8 orders *(Q4 FY26, shareholder_letter, p.13)* — *"top 10% of monthly users placed around 8 orders"*
- **InstaHelp top 1% monthly users orders in March 2026 *(excluded category)*:** around 21 orders *(Q4 FY26, shareholder_letter, p.13)* — *"top 1% placed around 21 orders"*

### 2. Core Consumer Services vs. InstaHelp
- InstaHelp contributed 2.7 million orders and ₹40 cr NTV in Q4, with March alone crossing 1.1 million orders *(Q4 FY26, earnings_transcript, p.3)* — *"A business that didn't exist at the start of FY26 delivered 2.7 million orders and ₹40 crores of NTV in Q4 — with March alone crossing 1.1 million orders"*
- Both existing Urban Company consumers and new first‑time users are adopting InstaHelp *(Q4 FY26, earnings_transcript, p.17)* — *"we're seeing both existing cohorts of Urban Company consumers adopt it, but we're also seeing newer cohorts and newer customers, first time to the platform, adopt InstaHelp"*
- Management indicated cross‑utilization is bidirectional: InstaHelp users also use core consumer services and vice‑versa *(Q4 FY26, earnings_transcript, p.25)* — *"are we seeing incrementally, any new users coming solely for InstaHelp, and again, you're able to cross‑utilize it for consumers, or it's the other way around? ... It's both."*

### 3. Category & Seasonality Patterns
- High‑frequency subscription users drive repeat deep‑cleaning weekly or fortnightly in UAE and Singapore *(Q4 FY26, earnings_transcript, p.24)* — *"many of our users use our deep cleaning ... on a weekly or fortnightly basis, and quite a few of them use it multiple times a week"*
- International users have 2.6x higher annual transaction frequency than India consumer services users, growing ~15% YoY *(Q4 FY26, shareholder_letter, p.10)* — *"annual transaction frequency for International users was 2.6x that of India consumer services users in FY26, and grew ~15% YoY"*

### 4. Cities & Customer Segments
- India consumer services NTV grew 26% YoY in Q4, fastest in 11 quarters, with top 10 cities growing 25% and remaining cities 27% YoY *(Q4 FY26, shareholder_letter, p.9)* — *"Growth was strong across all cities - top 10 cities grew 25% and remaining cities grew 27% YoY"*
- First‑time users in India grew 19% YoY across both top 10 and other cities *(Q4 FY26, shareholder_letter, p.9)* — *"First-time users ... grew 19% YoY across both the Top 10 and remaining cities"*

### 5. Customer Experience & Product Levers
- Differentiated product offerings (upgraded cleaning, expanded beauty, Foamjet AC) strengthened word‑of‑mouth and new user acquisition *(Q4 FY26, shareholder_letter, p.11)* — *"upgraded cleaning experiences, an expanded beauty portfolio in the UAE, and Foamjet AC servicing in Singapore - has strengthened word of mouth and supported new user acquisition"*
- Noon partnership in UAE emerged as a consistently performing consumer acquisition channel *(Q4 FY26, shareholder_letter, p.11)* — *"In the UAE specifically, the Noon partnership has emerged as an additional and consistently performing consumer acquisition channel"*
- Platform densification into smaller micro‑markets reduced fulfilment times and improved consumer conversion *(Q4 FY26, shareholder_letter, p.10)* — *"Moving to smaller, tighter micro-markets with high demand has reduced fulfilment times and improved consumer conversion"*

### 6. Gaps / Blind Spots (What Filings Do NOT Explain)
- Management did not disclose detailed churn rates, lifetime value segmentation, or the specific impact of subscription vs. on‑demand users on margin expansion; customer interviews should probe actual retention beyond the 30‑33% monthly figure and explore satisfaction drivers for high‑frequency users.

## Q1 FY27
### 1. Repeat & Retention Metrics
- **Net Transaction Value (NTV) growth YoY (consolidated):** 42% YoY *(Q1 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.3)* — *"On a consolidated basis, NTV grew 42% YoY to ₹1,465 Cr."*
- **Annual transacting users growth YoY:** ~21% YoY to ~8.2 million *(Q1 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.5)* — *"Annual transacting users grew ~21% YoY to ~8.2 million"*
- **First‑time users growth YoY:** ~24% YoY to ~0.92 million *(Q1 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.5)* — *"First time users grew ~24% YoY to ~0.92 million"*
- **NTV per annual transacting user increase:** ~7% YoY (₹1,205 to ₹1,293) *(Q1 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.5)* — *"NTV per annual transacting user rose ~7% YoY, from ~₹1,205 to ~₹1,293"*
- **Adjusted EBITDA margin (core India services excl. InstaHelp):** 6.9% of NTV *(Q1 FY27, earnings_transcript, p.3)* — *"Adjusted EBITDA margin was 6.9% of NTV"*

### 2. Core Consumer Services vs. InstaHelp
- Core India Consumer Services (excluding InstaHelp) NTV grew 29% YoY, reaching ₹1,056 Cr, first quarter over ₹1,000 Cr. *(Q1 FY27, earnings_transcript, p.3)* — *"India Consumer Services, excluding InstaHelp, grew 29% in NTV year-on-year to reach ₹1,056 crores"*
- InstaHelp contributed a ₹(132) Cr investment, pulling Adjusted EBITDA to a loss of ₹(65) Cr on a consolidated basis. *(Q1 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.3)* — *"Adjusted EBITDA for the quarter was ₹(65) Cr., reflecting a ₹(132) Cr. investment in InstaHelp"*
- Adjusted EBITDA margin for India Consumer Services (Ex InstaHelp) improved ~170 bps YoY to 6.9% of NTV. *(Q1 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.6)* — *"Adjusted EBITDA margin for India Consumer Services (Ex InstaHelp) expanded ~170 bps YoY to 6.9% of NTV"*

### 3. Category & Seasonality Patterns
- Q1 is seasonally the strongest quarter for margins; supply onboarding and marketing investments occur in the prior quarter. *(Q1 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.6)* — *"Q1 is seasonally our strongest quarter for margins, as supply onboarding and marketing investments largely sit in the preceding quarter"*
- Growth was broad‑based across beauty, cleaning, pest control, repairs, and handyman, not reliant on a single category. *(Q1 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.5)* — *"Growth was broad based across beauty, cleaning and pest control, repairs, and handyman, rather than reliant on any single category"*

### 4. Cities & Customer Segments
- Tier 2 cities (beyond top 10 metros) NTV grew ~36% YoY, outpacing top 10 cities (+28.7%). *(Q1 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.6)* — *"NTV for Tier 2 cities ... grew ~36% YoY in Q1 FY27, outpacing the top 10 cities"*
- A rising share of new customers now comes from Tier 2 cities. *(Q1 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.6)* — *"A rising share of new customers now comes from these cities"*

### 5. Customer Experience & Product Levers
- AI‑led savings in customer and partner support contributed to contribution margin expansion. *(Q1 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.6)* — *"contribution margin expansion, driven mainly by AI led savings in customer and partner support"*
- UC Instant rollout reduced fulfillment time to 30‑60 minutes for core services, boosting word‑of‑mouth and retention. *(Q1 FY27, earnings_transcript, p.5)* — *"roll out UC Instant, which is basically getting all our core services ... between 30 to 60 minutes"*

### 6. Gaps / Blind Spots (What Filings Do NOT Explain)
- Management does not disclose detailed repeat‑purchase frequency metrics for InstaHelp beyond qualitative statements, nor does it provide granular churn or tenure data for partners; these gaps must be explored through customer reviews and partner interviews.

## Q2 FY27
### 1. Repeat & Retention Metrics
- **Annual transacting users grew:** 22.4% YoY to 8.6 million *(Q2 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.7)* — *"Annual transacting users grew 22.4% YoY to 8.6 million"*
- **First‑time users grew:** 28% YoY to 0.76 million *(Q2 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.7)* — *"first-time users grew 28% YoY to 0.76 million"*
- **NTV per annual transacting user:** up 3.2% YoY *(Q2 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.7)* — *"NTV per annual transacting user up 3.2% YoY"*

### 2. Core Consumer Services vs. InstaHelp
- InstaHelp orders grew 63% QoQ to 6.2‑6.23 million, making it the highest‑frequency offering *(Q2 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.5)* — *"InstaHelp's growth accelerated, with order volumes up 63% QoQ to 6.2 million"*
- In micro‑markets where InstaHelp runs alongside core services, roughly 45% of new users come through InstaHelp *(Q2 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.12)* — *"roughly 45% of new users to the platform now come through InstaHelp"*
- Core India Consumer Services (ex‑InstaHelp) NTV grew 26.3% YoY to ₹963 Cr *(Q2 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.7)* — *"India Consumer Services (Ex InstaHelp) NTV grew 26.3% YoY to ₹963 Cr"*

### 3. Category & Seasonality Patterns
- Penetration in largest categories in top 8 cities remains below 10%, indicating headroom *(Q2 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.3)* — *"Penetration in our largest categories in the Top 8 cities remains below 10%"*
- Tier‑2 cities growing ~37% YoY, emerging as a second growth engine *(Q2 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.3)* — *"Tier 2 cities, growing 37% YoY, are emerging as a second growth engine"*
- Festive demand shifted entirely to Q3, so Q2 growth is not seasonally boosted *(Q2 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.7)* — *"this year shifting entirely to Q3"*

### 4. Cities & Customer Segments
- Top 8 cities hold the largest category penetration but still under 10% *(Q2 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.3)* — *"Penetration in our largest categories in the Top 8 cities remains below 10%"*
- Tier‑2 markets are growing faster and receiving wider service assortment *(Q2 FY27, earnings_transcript, p.5)* — *"Tier 2 is now growing much faster, ... users getting a wider assortment of services"*
- International markets grew 66% YoY, with UAE performing strongly despite external challenges *(Q2 FY27, earnings_transcript, p.3)* — *"we grew at 66.2% year‑on‑year. UAE is a very large market for us, and despite the war, it has continued to grow very well"*

### 5. Customer Experience & Product Levers
- Instant availability (30‑60 min) is being rolled out, showing lower cancellation rates and higher partner utilisation where available *(Q2 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.8)* — *"Cancellation rates on Instant jobs are lower, and partner utilisation has improved"*
- Densification reduces travel between jobs, lowering per‑order support costs and improving adjusted EBITDA loss per order *(Q2 FY27, shareholder_letter, p.5)* — *"Adjusted EBITDA loss per order improved ... driven by densification: more orders per micro‑market, shorter travel between jobs"*
- Training, SOPs, tooling and AI‑driven quality control are cited as drivers of word‑of‑mouth acquisition *(Q2 FY27, earnings_transcript, p.5)* — *"we've continued to make meaningful investments in training, in SOPs, in tooling, and quality control via AI, and that compounds the word of mouth faster"*

### 6. Gaps / Blind Spots (What Filings Do NOT Explain)
- Management does not disclose quantitative retention rates (e.g., repeat purchase frequency, churn) or the impact of Instant availability on user repeat behavior, which must be uncovered through customer reviews and interviews.
